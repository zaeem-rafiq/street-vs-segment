# Features

What Street vs. Segment does today, in plain words, and the test that proves each feature. Proof names a test file and, after `#`, the name of the test inside it. The first proof is the feature's end-to-end test.

| Feature | What it does | How to reach it | Proof | Broken looks like |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gap cards | The home page shows one card per saved research run. Each card shows the market's Yes price, the segment's Yes share labeled "AI panel", the gap as a signed number of points with its direction, and an average-gap line above the cards | Open the site's home page | `e2e/home.spec.ts#shows one gap card per saved run with market, segment and signed gap`, `src/lib/gap.test.ts#always equals the displayed segment minus the displayed market` | No cards, a card missing a number, an unsigned gap, or a gap that does not equal the two numbers subtracted |
| Market detail | Opening a card shows who was asked (Census profile), the questions Flashpoint asked, and at least three quotes, with the full AI-panel disclosure | Click any card | `e2e/home.spec.ts#opens a market to show who was asked, the questions and their quotes` | A blank or error page, or a missing panel or quote |
| Saved-price fallback | When Kalshi cannot be reached, each card shows the price saved with the run and says "Saved price", and the top bar says "Some prices are saved, not live" instead of "Kalshi prices read just now" | Happens on its own when Kalshi is down | `e2e/home.spec.ts#shows the saved price when Kalshi cannot be reached`, `src/lib/kalshi.test.ts#returns the saved price when the network fails`, `src/components/SiteHeader.test.tsx#never calls a saved price live` | An error page, a blank price, or a saved price labeled as live |
| Phone layout | The page fits a 375-pixel phone screen with no sideways scrolling | Open the site on a phone | `e2e/home.spec.ts#fits a 375-pixel phone screen without sideways scrolling` | The page scrolls sideways or text is cut off |
| Known-answer check | When a run has a known-answer check, its home card shows one line: the known answer, the panel's Yes share, how many points that is from the truth, and a link to the source. The market page shows the full question, the fact, and how many points the panel missed the truth by | Open the home page; click a card that has the line | `e2e/home.spec.ts#shows the known-answer check on the card and in full on the market page` | The line is missing on a run that has the check, the source link is broken, or the miss is not the panel's distance from 0% or 100% |
| Who drives the gap | When a run has breakdowns, the market page shows each group (by age, region or gender) with its size and Yes share as a bar, with the Kalshi price marked on every bar | Click a card whose run has breakdowns | `e2e/home.spec.ts#shows who drives the gap with each group's Yes share` | The section is missing, a group is missing, or a share is wrong |
| Strike ladder | When a run has the panel's own estimates, the market page lists every Kalshi strike with the market's Yes price next to the share of panel estimates above that strike, and the panel's median estimate. Strikes where both values sit within 2 points of 0% or 100% are hidden and counted. When Kalshi's strikes cannot be loaded, it says so instead of showing an empty chart | Click a card whose run has estimates | `e2e/home.spec.ts#shows the strike ladder section, and says why when Kalshi's strikes cannot be loaded` | The section is missing, a row shows a share that does not match the estimates, or an empty chart appears with no message |
