Kalshi gives US gas above $3.70 on November 30 a 34% chance. A Flashpoint.AI panel of 100 US drivers says 59%. That 25-point gap is what Street vs. Segment finds, and across our 3 markets the panel and the market differ by 17 points on average.

Built today at Hack the Unreachable. For any Kalshi economics market, Claude works out who lives the outcome, Flashpoint runs a 100-persona panel of them, and the page puts their answer next to the live price, with each Kalshi strike, the panel's own estimate, and which groups drive the gap.

Then we test our own panel on questions whose answers are already known. It misses them by 41 to 76 points. So these gaps are leads to check, not trades, and the next step is one Flashpoint call away: real respondents.
