# Demo script (2 minutes)

Read the gap numbers off the live page just before presenting. Kalshi prices move, so the numbers below are placeholders in brackets.

| Time | Say | Screen (real product?) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | "Markets tell you what's priced in. They don't tell you what the people who live the outcome expect." | Home page, gap cards already showing (real) |
| 0:05 | "Kalshi says [56]% that New York rents rise more than 4%. New York renters say [30]%. That's a [26]-point gap." Point at the big red number. | First gap card (real) |
| 0:30 | "So we built Street vs. Segment. You pick a Kalshi economics market. Claude works out who lives that outcome. Flashpoint pulls their Census profile, writes the survey and runs a panel matched to it. We put their answer next to the price." | Click into the card: who was asked, the questions (real) |
| 1:00 | "Here's what they said, in their words." Read one quote. | Answers panel with quotes (real) |
| 1:15 | "Across [three] markets, the segment and the market disagree by an average of [N] points." | Back to home, average line (real) |
| 1:35 | "The best signal sits with people no trader asks. Now an agent asks them before the market moves." | Home page |
| 1:50 | "Today's panels are AI personas checked against Census data. Swapping in real respondents is one Flashpoint call. We left it out for budget, not because it's hard." | Footer disclosure (real) |

## Before going on stage

1. Open https://street-vs-segment.onrender.com about two minutes early. The free plan sleeps and can take up to a minute to wake.
2. Read the three gaps and the average off the page and say those numbers.
